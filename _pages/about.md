I am a hardware security researcher and security architect with more than a decade of experience in fault injection, side-channel analysis, cryptography, and the security of AI models on embedded devices. My work connects implementation-level security research with the engineering of secure systems.

I am currently a **Principal Security Architect at [qtlabs](https://www.qtlabs.at/)** in Vienna, where I focus on the security architecture and practical security of quantum communication systems, including quantum key distribution (QKD). This work brings together my interests in cryptography, physical attacks, threat modeling, and the gap between theoretical security guarantees and real-world implementations.

### Research & Expertise

* **Hardware and Implementation Security:** Fault injection attacks (FIA), side-channel analysis (SCA), and security evaluation of cryptographic and neural-network implementations on embedded devices.
* **Cryptography and Quantum Communication:** Symmetric and post-quantum cryptography, secure implementations, and the practical security of QKD systems and their classical components.
* **Security Architecture and Assurance:** Threat modeling, security-by-design, and implementation assurance for complex systems, from automotive electronics to quantum communication infrastructure.

My research includes contributions to cryptographic primitives such as DEFAULT, Baksheesh, and ASCON-SIGN. I also co-authored *Cryptography and Embedded Systems Security* (Springer, 2024) and co-edited *Automated Methods in Cryptographic Fault Analysis* (Springer, 2019).

### Professional Background

Before joining qtlabs, I was **Senior Cybersecurity Manager at TTControl** in Vienna, working on product security and ISO/SAE 21434 across embedded control systems. Earlier, I served as a **Senior Scientist at Silicon Austria Labs**, **Principal Research Fellow at HP-NTU Digital Manufacturing Corporate Lab**, and held research and security-evaluation roles at **Underwriters Laboratories (UL)** and **Temasek Laboratories** in Singapore.

I hold a Ph.D. in Applied Informatics and am a **Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP)** and **Certified Automotive Cybersecurity Professional (CACSP)**. Across both research and industry, I am interested in understanding how systems fail under realistic attacks and in translating that understanding into more secure designs.
