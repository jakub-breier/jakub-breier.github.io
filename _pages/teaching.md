My teaching focuses on cryptography, practical hardware security, and secure embedded systems. I aim to connect the principles of security with experimental evaluation and real-world implementations.

## Courses

**Cryptography and Embedded Systems Security**  
Graduate course, Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava (2022–present).

The course covers side-channel analysis, fault injection, secure cryptographic implementations, and countermeasures.

**Cryptography and Embedded Systems Security — online course**  
EC-Council (2025–present). A five-hour on-demand course covering selected topics from the university course. [Course website](https://codered.eccouncil.org/course/cryptography-and-embedded-systems-security).

**Team Project**  
Graduate course, Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava (2024–present). Two-semester student projects in applied security, including the development of a laser fault injection station (2024/2025) and an intelligent automated threat analysis and risk assessment framework for automotive security (2025/2026).
